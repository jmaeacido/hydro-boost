FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Hydro Boost?

Hydro Boost is an electrolyte gummy that delivers the hydration benefits of a full sports drink in a convenient, chewable gummy. It provides essential electrolytes that help replenish what your body loses through sweat and daily activity, without the need for bottles, powders, or mixing. Whether you're working out, traveling, working outdoors, or simply staying active, Hydro Boost makes hydration simple, portable, and enjoyable.

Who should use Hydro Boost?

Hydro Boost is designed for anyone who wants a convenient way to maintain proper hydration. It is especially beneficial for athletes, fitness enthusiasts, runners, hikers, cyclists, outdoor workers, frequent travelers, and individuals with busy lifestyles who need a quick and effective hydration solution without carrying sports drinks everywhere.

What are the benefits of taking Hydro Boost?

Hydro Boost helps replenish essential electrolytes that support proper hydration and fluid balance. Staying hydrated helps your body perform at its best, whether you're exercising, working, traveling, or enjoying outdoor activities. Its portable gummy format also makes it easier to stay consistent with your hydration routine wherever life takes you.

How is Hydro Boost different from traditional sports drinks?

Hydro Boost provides the essential electrolytes your body needs in a compact gummy instead of a large bottle of liquid. Unlike many traditional sports drinks that contain high amounts of sugar and require you to carry bulky bottles, Hydro Boost offers a cleaner, more convenient way to stay hydrated. You simply chew a gummy whenever you need hydration support, making it ideal for active lifestyles and people on the go.

When should I take Hydro Boost?

Hydro Boost can be taken before, during, or after physical activity to help support hydration. It is also an excellent choice while traveling, working outdoors, spending time in hot weather, or anytime you need to replenish electrolytes and maintain hydration throughout the day.

Does it replace water?

No — it's a hydration companion. Drink water throughout the day, especially during training.

What electrolytes does Hydro Boost contain?

Hydro Boost contains three essential electrolytes: sodium, potassium, and magnesium. These minerals play an important role in maintaining fluid balance, supporting normal muscle function, and helping your body replace electrolytes lost through sweat and physical activity.

Does Hydro Boost contain sugar?

No. Hydro Boost contains 0 grams of sugar, making it a smart alternative to many traditional sports drinks that rely on added sugars. You receive the hydration support your body needs without the sugar overload or the crash that often follows sugary beverages.

What flavor is it?

Lemon lime with a bright citrus bite — no chalky finish.

Is Hydro Boost easy to carry while traveling or exercising?

Absolutely. Hydro Boost is specifically designed for portability. Its gummy format fits easily into your gym bag, backpack, purse, or pocket, allowing you to enjoy hydration support anytime without carrying heavy bottles or measuring powders.

Is it gym bag ready?

Yes. Pocket-sized, no mixing, no spills, no shaker required.
